## R4d experiment

- Store signed integrated U/V transfer (dt*h*meanOpening*intervalVelocity, m² for unit depth) separately from candidate endpoint-open fluid velocity. Closed endpoint faces retain historical transfer but have zero endpoint *fluid* velocity. This is not full MAC velocity: a closed face's solid boundary speed is a different quantity. No division by endpoint aperture.
- Independently consume stored U/V transfer in deterministic row-major U then V order to rebuild the compatibility-corrected volume ledger; require exact numerical equality to R4b per-cell residual. Emit both arrays, transfer/endpoint maxima and counts. Retain all previously emitted rows.
- On each of five frozen candidate solutions (index 43 resolutions 8/32, index 42 resolution 64, index 44 refined depths 12/16), run a test-only simultaneous donor-limited scalar transport consumer twice: uniform concentration one in previous fluid volume and actual frozen seeded tracer mass. Use signed integrated transfers once, including end-closed faces. Each donor concentration is based on its start capacity and all outgoing requests are scaled together if requests exceed capacity; the remaining-mass cap is a roundoff guard. No swept remap, mass deletion or forced evacuation. Require finite nonnegative mass and total mass conservation within 1e-12*max(1,initialMass). Record donor volume shortfall, mass moved across closed faces, trapped/closing-cell mass, and uniform concentration error against endpoint geometric volume. The donor limiter acts on tracer only, not fluid transfer; it can prevent exact constant-concentration preservation or trap mass, especially if fluid traverses several cells during the interval. These are explicit blockers, not waived correctness checks.
- Emit 8 ledger fields, four arrays with sizes (5,532 rows), and two sets of 10 tracer diagnostics plus 1,345 mass array rows = 8,250 per solution, 41,250 exact rows. Existing 140 intervalHandoff, 56 spaceTimeProjection and topology witnesses retained. With one missed cell, expected total 7,721,613 Fluid/diagnostic rows.

## Acceptance and limits

Review R4c final gate, R4d producers, strict compare and full CMake. Compare the stored ledger, transfer on closed faces, tracer conservation, donor shortfall, trapped mass, and uniform-field error. A conserved total tracer mass is not sufficient for a physically consistent transport scheme; uniform concentration, local geometric conservation and topology transitions still matter. The current model holds body response and compatibility correction fixed, so actual end volume and model-corrected volume need not agree. No sequential candidate trajectory, newly opened face velocity reconstruction, predicted solid boundary synchronization, production tracer replacement or live World integration. If donor limitation/trapping is observed, use a time-resolved transport experiment rather than deleting mass or changing the fluid volume ledger. Master, #322 and #326 unchanged.
