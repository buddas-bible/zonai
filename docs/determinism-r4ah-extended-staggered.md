## Fourfold continuation extension

Extend only the optimized trajectory from 16 to 64 intervals (43–106 inclusive), preserving its bootstrap, numerical settings, source schedule, pressure history and previous 16 interval trace values by design. Keep the historical strict and matched-state comparisons unchanged. The test still reconstructs a free-body World each interval and cannot handle contacts or joints in this fixture. A constraint, nonanalytic geometry, unsupported volume, or solver failure remains a test failure, not a skipped step. Negative modeled volumes remain reported without clamping or fallback. This is an intermediate duration test rather than proof of long-term stability.

Audit committed MAC component peaks, a conservative component-wise CFL bound dt*(maxAbsU+maxAbsV)/h, body speed and angular speed, applied/next force magnitudes, actual body kinetic energy, geometric fluid volume and elapsed simulated time. Check each body's observed linear and angular momentum change against its applied lagged pressure impulse, reporting the maximum magnitude of each discrepancy. This is a free-body impulse accounting diagnostic, not a closed fluid/rigid-body momentum or energy budget. The fluid is driven by an external source, and endpoint projection has a distinct momentum proxy.
