## Experiment

- Preserve prior pressure quadrature, all legacy diagnostic outputs and R4d simultaneous tracer consumer. Retain the midpoint effective apertures for each pressure panel; reconstruct actual panel-start fluid fractions on a separate geometry copy, using the same interpolated poses (first panel uses exact stored previous fractions). No modification of the actual pressure field or production code.
- Use the same solved constant interval face velocity on every panel. Signed panel transfer = dt*h*(panelWeight/weightSum)*midpointAperture*intervalVelocity. Require the per-face sum to reproduce the original integrated transfer ledger within relative roundoff tolerance. Keep transfers on end-closed faces.
- Advance uniform and actual seeded mass chronologically through all panels, with simultaneous per-panel donor limiting based on start geometric capacity and current tracer mass. Newly received mass becomes available in the next panel, not within the same panel. Require nonnegative finite mass and total mass conservation at every panel. Report limited donor-panel events, unserved volume, mass crossing closed faces, trapped/closing mass and maximum endpoint concentration. Do not erase trapped mass or apply a second swept remap.
- Independently reconstruct a flux-only endpoint model volume (start volume plus signed net interval inflow), without adding any scalar source for pressure compatibility or predicted body displacement. Record its discrepancy from actual endpoint geometry and any negative model volume. For uniform tracer, report errors against both geometric and flux-only volume; this separates transport error from the pressure model/geometry inconsistency. The flux-only volume may be negative and is a diagnostic, not a physical capacity used for division.
- Emit 8 common fields and two sets of 12 fields plus 1,345 mass-vector rows per solution: 2,722 per solution, 13,610 new chronologicalTracer rows. Five frozen cases unchanged. Expected total 7,735,223 Fluid/diagnostic rows with one historical missed cell.

## Acceptance and limits

Both platform producers, strict comparison and full CMake required. Compare R4d vs R4e donor shortfall, uniform errors and trapped mass; no presumption that chronological transport improves every metric. A constant interval velocity and midpoint quadrature do not guarantee per-panel geometric conservation, and current rigid-response/compatibility source can differ from actual endpoint volume. No temporal pressure reconstruction, no actual endpoint MAC state, no live candidate trajectory, no production tracer integration or performance claim. If local errors persist, investigate discrete geometric conservation with consistent predicted boundary motion rather than imposing scalar corrections or deleting mass.
