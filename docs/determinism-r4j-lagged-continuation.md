## Acceptance and limits

This is the first persistent **lagged** candidate continuation, not a fully consistent forward-time space-time integrator. Historical pressure response is applied to the next World interval; no corrected physical path rebuild or tracer transport. Candidate pressure is an interval pressure, not a verified instantaneous endpoint pressure. Nonnegative flux volume and low endpoint continuity are diagnostic, not guaranteed. Additional production projection and repeated quadrature/reference PCG make this unsuitable as a performance benchmark. Both producers, strict comparison and full CMake required; examine whether the initial spike reduction persists and whether any new spike, force drift or support failure appears. If unsuitable, do not promote the algorithm: use observed failure to decide whether a simpler stable alternative or a forward predictor/corrector is justified. No production changes, defaults, clamps or master merge; #322/#326 unchanged.
