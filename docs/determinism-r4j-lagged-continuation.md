## Acceptance and limits

This is the first persistent **lagged** candidate continuation, not a fully consistent forward-time space-time integrator. Historical pressure response is applied to the next World interval; no corrected physical path rebuild or tracer transport. Candidate pressure is an interval pressure, not a verified instantaneous endpoint pressure. Nonnegative flux volume and low endpoint continuity are diagnostic, not guaranteed. Additional production projection and repeated quadrature/reference PCG make this unsuitable as a performance benchmark. Both producers, strict comparison and full CMake required; examine whether the initial spike reduction persists and whether any new spike, force drift or support failure appears. If unsuitable, do not promote the algorithm: use observed failure to decide whether a simpler stable alternative or a forward predictor/corrector is justified. No production changes, defaults, clamps or master merge; #322/#326 unchanged.

### Proposed R4k: same-interval forward prediction and measured volume audit

Before adding more persistent intervals or nonlinear iterations, compare the lagged full-response baseline with one independently replayed forward predictor on the same initial pre-pressure states. Capture the actual start World state; use an independent World copy to predict the next endpoint without candidate pressure, preserving the interval duration, source schedule, mass/inertia, and two-substep cadence. Form the mean apertures, coupling coefficients and geometric volume change over that predicted *forward* interval. Evaluate the existing full-response candidate once, apply the selected pressure loads to a second fresh World from the same start, and rebuild the actual corrected endpoint geometry. Do not apply reference projection or body loads twice to a committed state. Keep this test-only; do not claim a valid integrator if pressure/geometry time levels cannot be made consistent.

For each of 43..46, distinguish the signed chronological transfer ledger from instantaneous endpoint velocities. Measure actual per-cell corrected endpoint volume change versus integrated flux (raw L1, max local residual, compatibility correction, true projected residual, negative modeled volume); separately report old endpoint continuity, peak interval and endpoint speeds, force/torque and support failures. Include actual World position/angle and geometry closure; preserve initial production replay, deterministic ordering and all previous strict trace rows. If corrected geometry leaves the predicted support or requires source deletion, report failure without a silent fallback. Record solve counts and quadrature cost before considering repeated predictor iterations. Use both platform producers, exact comparison and full regression. No new production path or master merge until evidence demonstrates a stable, conservative, affordable model; otherwise revisit a simpler game-oriented coupling scheme.
