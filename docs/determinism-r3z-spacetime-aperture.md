## Acceptance and limitations

Review R3y Windows first, then R3z producer logs, exact comparison and full regression. Compare quadrature convergence and actual cadence peaks, continuity, failure counts and force feedback; more substeps cost more and may simply shift the near-closure event. A time-averaged aperture bound is only a geometric feasibility diagnostic. A conservative space-time coupled operator would require consistent time-integrated face/solid coefficients, treatment of disappearing/reappearing control volumes and tracer transport, and dedicated conservation/force tests. No new production solver, sampling switch, default change, speed cap or pooling. Master and experimental #322/#326 untouched.
